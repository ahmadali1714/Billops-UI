1. Who We Are

BillOps ("we," "our," or "us") is a time-tracking and billing automation platform designed for service-based businesses. Our service connects your work hours to QuickBooks invoicing automatically. We are operated by Ahmad Ali, and can be reached at abubakarzech@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy applies to the BillOps web application and marketing website located at our registered domain.

2. Data We Collect

Account Information

When you create a BillOps account or sign in via OAuth, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Profile picture (if provided by your OAuth provider)
  • Company or organization name
  • Role or job title (optional)

Time-Tracking Data

  • Timer start and stop timestamps
  • Duration of work sessions
  • Client, job, and service type associations
  • Notes and tags you add to time entries
  • Date-range filters and exported reports

Usage Data

  • Log data (browser type, IP address, pages visited, timestamps)
  • Device and operating system information
  • Crash reports and error logs

Communications

If you contact us via the website contact form, we collect your name, email address, company name, and message content in order to respond to your inquiry.

3. Google OAuth Data

Google API Services User Data Policy: BillOps's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

When you choose to connect your Google account, BillOps requests only the permissions necessary to provide the service. Depending on the features you use, we may request access to:

Scopes We Request

  • openid / profile / email — To authenticate your identity and display your name and email in the app.
  • Google Calendar (read-only) — To optionally pull calendar event data so you can associate time entries with scheduled meetings or work blocks. We read event titles and durations only.

What We Do With Google Data

  • We use Google account data solely to provide the features you explicitly enable.
  • We do not use Google data to serve ads or build advertising profiles.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your Google data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • We do not use Google data to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to any other party except as necessary to provide the service (e.g., storing your time entries in our secure database).

Revoking Google Access

You can disconnect your Google account at any time from within BillOps account settings, or directly from your Google Account Permissions page. After disconnection, we delete your Google OAuth tokens from our systems within 30 days.

4. Microsoft / Outlook OAuth Data

When you connect your Microsoft or Outlook account, BillOps may request:

  • openid / profile / email — For authentication and identity display.
  • Calendars.Read — To optionally read calendar events for time-entry association, identical in purpose to the Google Calendar scope above.

The same data-use principles apply: we use Microsoft data only to provide the features you enable, we do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising. You may revoke access at any time via Microsoft's app permissions page or from within BillOps settings.

5. QuickBooks OAuth Data

BillOps's core function is to push time-entry data into QuickBooks Online. When you connect QuickBooks, we access:

  • Your QuickBooks company ID and company name
  • Client and customer lists (read)
  • Job and project lists (read)
  • Service item lists (read)
  • Invoices (create, on your behalf, when a timer stops)
  • Time activity records (create and read)

We use this data exclusively to automate the billing workflow you have configured. We never modify or delete existing QuickBooks records except as a direct result of your own actions in BillOps. You can revoke QuickBooks access from within BillOps or from your Intuit account settings.

6. How We Use Your Data

  • Service delivery — Tracking time, generating invoices, and syncing data with connected services.
  • Authentication — Verifying your identity and maintaining your session.
  • Customer support — Responding to inquiries and resolving issues.
  • Security and fraud prevention — Detecting and investigating unauthorized access.
  • Product improvement — Analyzing aggregate, anonymized usage patterns to improve the service. We do not use identifiable data for this purpose.
  • Legal compliance — Meeting obligations under applicable law.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data to any third party.

7. Data Sharing

We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your connected services — Data is sent to QuickBooks, Google, or Microsoft only as a direct result of actions you take in BillOps.
  • Infrastructure providers — We use trusted cloud hosting and database providers (e.g., AWS, Vercel) under strict data-processing agreements. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it independently.
  • Legal requirements — If required by law, court order, or governmental authority, we may disclose data as necessary. We will notify you to the extent permitted by law.
  • Business transfer — In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred. We will notify affected users and provide an opportunity to delete their data before any transfer occurs.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Specifically:

  • Active accounts — Time entries, billing records, and account data are retained indefinitely while your account is active so your billing history is always accessible.
  • Deleted accounts — We delete or anonymize all personal data within 30 days of account deletion, except where retention is required by law (e.g., financial records for tax purposes, which may be retained for up to 7 years).
  • OAuth tokens — Access tokens for Google, Microsoft, and QuickBooks are stored encrypted and deleted within 30 days of disconnection.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — Request that inaccurate data be corrected.
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability — Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdrawal of consent — Revoke connected service access at any time without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at abubakarzech@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data is transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.
  • Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and never logged in plaintext.
  • Access to production data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • We perform regular security reviews and maintain an incident response plan.

In the event of a data breach that affects your personal data, we will notify affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by applicable law.

11. Children's Privacy

BillOps is designed for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 16. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a child, we will delete it promptly. Contact us at abubakarzech@gmail.com if you believe this has occurred.
