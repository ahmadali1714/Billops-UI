How It Works

Simple. Fast. Fully Automated.

Four steps is all it takes. BillOps handles the rest — no manual work, no missed billing.

1

Pick a Client & Job

Select the client and job from a dropdown — synced directly from your QuickBooks account. Always up to date.

2

Choose a Service

Select what type of work is being done — consulting, development, support — straight from your QuickBooks service list.

3

Start the Timer

Hit Start when work begins and Stop when done. Or enter hours manually. Both work — your choice, every time.

4

BillOps Does the Rest

Stop the timer — that's it. BillOps instantly saves your hours, calculates daily totals, and fires a complete invoice directly into QuickBooks. No one on your team needs to do a single thing.
